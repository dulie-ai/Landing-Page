Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026.

Dulie is a planning assistant on Telegram for events, tasks, reminders, and notes, with optional Google Calendar and Google Tasks connections. This policy explains what information we handle, why we handle it, and your choices.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Dulie is operated by Wang Rui Duan, located in Singapore ("Dulie," "we," "us"). This operator is responsible for personal information processed to provide Dulie.

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact dulie.business@gmail.com. Wang Rui Duan monitors this inbox and handles privacy enquiries.

2. Information we collect

  • Telegram account information: your Telegram user and chat identifiers, username, and display name, as provided to the bot.
  • Messages and saved content: text you send to Dulie, bot replies, conversation history, unfinished requests, event and task titles, note content, dates, recurrence rules, reminder settings, and records of actions such as edits and undo requests. Content may include information about other people that you choose to provide.
  • Preferences: language, timezone, event categories, default durations, notification preferences, and other settings you configure. A location you type to set your timezone may also appear in your message history.
  • Search information: numerical representations of item titles, called embeddings, associated with saved items to find similar entries. Search text is processed to find matches. These representations are treated as user data, not as anonymous information.
  • Google connection information: if you connect Google, authorization tokens, granted permissions, token expiry, and identifiers that link Dulie items to Google calendars, task lists, events, and tasks. Google also returns event or task details in responses to the operations we request.
  • Technical and support information: service errors, request times, information you send when asking for help, and connection information such as IP addresses and browser details that infrastructure providers may receive when serving the website or API.

Dulie does not ask for your Google password. The current Google connection does not request Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or Google profile permissions. Telegram sends the messages made available to the bot; Dulie does not receive your entire personal Telegram chat history.

3. How we use information

We use information to identify your Dulie account, interpret messages, maintain conversation context, create and find saved items, process edits and deletions, send reminders and summaries, apply your preferences, and perform Google sync actions you request. We also use necessary information to answer support requests, diagnose failures, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations.

Providing message content and Telegram identifiers is necessary to use the bot. Connecting Google is optional. Declining or disconnecting Google prevents Google sync but does not prevent use of Dulie's local planning features.

4. Google Calendar and Google Tasks

When you choose to connect Google, the authorization screen requests the following permissions. Their technical reach is broader than the operations Dulie currently performs:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: access to view and modify events on your calendars. Dulie uses this to create, update, and delete events associated with Dulie in your primary calendar.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks: access to view and manage your tasks and task lists. Dulie uses this to create, update, and delete tasks associated with Dulie in your default task list.

We send the relevant titles, descriptions or task notes, dates, times, recurrence details, and event colors to Google. We receive operation responses and retain the identifiers needed for later sync. The current integration does not list or import your existing Google events or tasks. A connected account may also receive existing Dulie items when the sync flow runs.

Tokens are stored in our database so Dulie can continue authorized sync without asking you to sign in each time. They are not included in AI prompts. Disconnecting Google removes the stored tokens used for future requests; it does not erase your Dulie history, existing Google items, or necessarily stop a request already in progress.

Limited Use commitment

Dulie's handling of information obtained through Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and applicable Google Workspace user data rules, including their Limited Use restrictions.

We use Google data only for the visible features you authorize. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Transfers are limited to delivering authorized features with your consent, security, legal requirements, or a business transfer with your prior explicit consent. Human access is restricted to your affirmative permission for specific data, necessary security or legal purposes, or legally permitted aggregated and anonymized internal operations. These restrictions also apply to derived data and those acting for us.

5. AI processing and other recipients

  • OpenAI: Dulie sends your message, recent conversation context, relevant unfinished request details, and preferences to an AI model to interpret your request. Item titles and search text are also sent for embeddings. Content you type or paste about your calendar can therefore be processed by OpenAI even if Google is disconnected. The current integration does not send Google authorization tokens or import your Google calendar or task collection into AI prompts. OpenAI processes these requests as an external service provider. Provider-side processing and retention are governed by the applicable service arrangements; disconnecting Google does not stop AI processing of messages you send to Dulie.
  • Telegram: delivers your messages to the bot and receives Dulie's replies, reminders, and summaries. Telegram operates under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google: receives the event and task data needed for the optional sync you authorize and processes information within your Google account under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Timezone services: if you provide a city or place name, Dulie sends that text to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and the resulting coordinates to TimeAPI.io to resolve a timezone. A recognized timezone identifier can be applied without these lookups. These requests originate from Dulie's server; they do not request your device's GPS location.
  • Infrastructure: GitHub Pages hosts the public website. The team currently runs the bot and backend manually on team-operated computers, with records stored in the database used by that local setup. Dulie has not yet moved to a production server. We will update this policy when the hosting arrangements change.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We may disclose necessary information when legally required, to address abuse or security incidents, or in a reorganization subject to applicable law. The stricter Google data restrictions above apply whenever Google data is involved.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Messages, saved items, preferences, Google connection records, search representations, and action history are stored in Dulie's database. Using a few recent messages as AI context does not mean older messages are erased.

Deleting an item in the bot currently archives the local record; it is not permanent erasure. The ten-minute undo window is an action limit, not a retention deadline. An archived item, earlier conversation messages, action snapshots, or associated search data may remain until removed through a separate deletion process.

The current service has no automatic time-based cleanup of conversation history or archived items, and no self-service account-erasure feature. These records remain stored until manually removed. Expiry of an undo action or connection request does not by itself erase the underlying record.

Retention is limited by the purpose for which information is needed: saved items and settings support your use of Dulie; conversation history supports follow-up requests; technical records support troubleshooting and security. We will cease retaining personal information, or remove its association with you, when it is no longer needed for a business or legal purpose.

For permanent deletion, email the contact below. Rui Duan will review and verify the request, arrange manual removal of the relevant records, and explain any information that must be retained for a legal or security reason. We do not promise an automatic deletion deadline or a fixed backup-expiry period. We will tell you about applicable limitations and handle your request within the requirements of applicable law.

Account-erasure requests cover linked account records, messages, saved and archived items, search representations, pending requests, action history, settings, and tokens. Any retained copies must be considered when handling the request. Where retention is legally necessary, we will explain the reason where permitted and limit further use.

Deleting a Telegram conversation, blocking the bot, disconnecting Google, and erasing a Dulie account are separate actions. Dulie cannot erase copies independently controlled by Telegram or Google. Manage existing Google events and tasks in those services if you want them removed, especially after disconnecting.

7. Your choices and requests

Use /disconnect_google to remove Dulie's stored Google tokens. To revoke Google's authorization, also remove Dulie from your Google Account connections. You can stop using or block the Telegram bot at any time.

Contact dulie.business@gmail.com to request access, correction, a copy, or permanent deletion of your information. Include your Telegram username or identifier and the request; do not send passwords or Google tokens. We may ask for proportionate verification that you control the account before acting.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, transfer information, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Withdrawing consent does not undo earlier lawful processing. We will respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any lawful limits on a request.

8. Security and international processing

Dulie is currently operated in a local development setup. Google connections use OAuth authorization rather than your Google password, and requests to the configured Google, OpenAI, and timezone services use HTTPS. Stored messages and tokens are accessible to the operator's software; this is not an end-to-end encrypted storage service. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords, financial account credentials, or other highly sensitive information to the bot.

Telegram, Google, OpenAI, GitHub, and the timezone services may process information outside Singapore. Running the bot locally does not keep all information on the team's computers or within Singapore. Overseas transfers remain subject to applicable data protection requirements, including protection comparable to that required by Singapore's PDPA where it applies. Contact us for questions about a particular transfer or provider.

9. Website storage and links

The current Dulie website does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or application cookies. The hosting provider may retain technical access logs. Following links to Telegram, Google, GitHub, or other websites subjects your interactions there to those providers' practices. We will update this notice before introducing materially different tracking.

10. Age requirements

Dulie is intended for people aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Do not submit sensitive information about children or other people unless you have an appropriate legal basis.

11. Where a legal basis is required

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, core service processing is undertaken to provide the service you request; optional processing requiring consent relies on that consent; proportionate security and service administration may rely on legitimate interests where permitted; and legally required processing relies on the relevant obligation. We assess these bases against your rights. Google data remains subject to the narrower restrictions in section 4.

For Singapore privacy concerns, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission. You may also contact the relevant authority where you live if its laws apply.

12. Changes and contact

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. For material changes, we will provide a prominent notice through the website or bot before the change takes effect where required, and obtain fresh consent where needed. A policy update alone does not authorize a new use of Google data.

Privacy contact: dulie.business@gmail.com.
